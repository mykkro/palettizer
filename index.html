About this experiment

A revival of my old palette-warping tool. Image brightness selects a color; the six-point curve changes how those brightness values travel through the palette. Your images stay in this browser.

Load an image, choose a palette, and drag the curve points or use their sliders. Arrow keys move a focused curve point (Shift for larger steps). PNG export uses the mapped image even while Show original is enabled.

Plain JavaScript & Canvas. No uploads, no dependencies.Myrousz · MIT
